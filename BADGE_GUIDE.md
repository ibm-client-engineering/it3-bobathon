# 🎖️ Claiming Your Bobathon Badge

Completed the bobathon? Follow these steps to claim your IBM Bob badge through Credly.

---

> 💡 **Stay in the same conversation.** Claim your badge in the **same Bob chat you used for your labs** — do not open a new chat. Bob's Badge Issuer Lite mode reads your conversation history to evaluate what you completed. If you worked in a single conversation as recommended, that entire history is already there — Bob can see every prompt, every exercise, every checkpoint you worked through. Starting a new conversation means Bob has no context and the evaluation will require you to describe everything manually from scratch.

---

## Prerequisites

- You have completed the bobathon session
- You have an IBM Bob workspace open (this repo)
- You have a [Credly account](https://credly.com) or are happy to create one
- You have **Python** installed (pre-installed on macOS and Linux; on Windows, install from [python.org](https://www.python.org/downloads/) if needed — it's free and takes 2 minutes)

---

## 📝 Before You Start

Take 30 seconds to think about the following — Bob will ask you about these during the evaluation:

1. **Which labs did you complete?** (Lab 1, Lab 2, Lab 3, or some combination)
2. **What's one thing you learned or found surprising** about what Bob can do?
3. **What's one specific example** of something Bob did that saved you time?

Having these ready makes the evaluation faster and your badge justification stronger.

---

## Steps

### 1. Switch to Badge Issuer Lite mode

In IBM Bob, open the mode switcher and select **Badge Issuer Lite**.

> If you don't see it, ask your lab instructor to install it using the IBM Bob Badge Issuer extension.

### 2. Start the badge flow

Say something like:

> *"I'd like to claim my bobathon badge"*

Bob will load the badge catalogue and walk you through the evaluation.

> 💡 **Bob will ask you a few questions** before evaluating — including the event slug name. For this event the slug is: **`it3_bobathon`**. Have it ready.

### 3. Bob reviews your work

Bob will review your conversation history and check your work against the badge criteria. You may be asked to:

- Confirm what you built during the session
- Describe the problem you solved
- Add any missing detail about how you used IBM Bob

### 4. Confirm your details

Before issuing, Bob will ask you to confirm:

- **Your full name** — as you want it to appear on the certificate
- **Your Credly email** — the badge is delivered here, so make sure it's correct

### 5. Receive your badge

Once approved, Bob will:

1. Issue the badge via Credly — check your email to accept it
2. Generate an HTML certificate in `badge-issuer-output/` — open it in any browser

**To open the HTML certificate:**
In the Bob Explorer panel on the left, navigate to the `badge-issuer-output/` folder, right-click the `.html` file, and select **Reveal in File Explorer** (or **Open in Finder** on Mac). Then double-click the file to open it in your browser. Do not paste the file path directly into a browser address bar — it won't work that way.

---

## Tips

- The more detail you share about your work in the conversation, the smoother the evaluation
- IBM Bob will not issue a badge without explicit confirmation of your name and Credly email
- If you're unsure which badge applies, ask Bob — it will load the active catalogue and recommend the right one
- Certificates are saved locally in `badge-issuer-output/` — right-click the file in the Explorer panel and select **Reveal in File Explorer**, then double-click to open in a browser
- The event slug for this bobathon is **`it3_bobathon`** — Bob will ask for it during the flow
