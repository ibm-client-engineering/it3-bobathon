# Tone Reference Emails (Optional)

This folder is for Exercise 3 of Lab 1 — drafting an email in *your own* writing voice.

---

> ⚠️ **Do not add confidential, sensitive, or customer data.** Only use content you'd be comfortable sharing in a shared lab environment. When in doubt, use **Option 3** below — it requires no personal content at all.

---

## Choose your option

### Option 1 — Past emails you've written

Add **3–6 emails you've actually written** as separate `.md` or `.txt` files in this folder (e.g. `email-1.md`, `email-2.md`). Good candidates:
- A status update you sent to your manager or team
- A follow-up email to a vendor, client, or colleague
- Any routine email that reflects how you normally write

**How to add a file:**
1. In the Bob Explorer panel, right-click this folder (`tone-reference-emails/`)
2. Select **New File** and name it `email-1.md` (or similar)
3. Paste your email text in and save (`Cmd+S` / `Ctrl+S`)

> **Note:** `.eml` and `.msg` files from Outlook are **not supported**. Copy and paste the email text into a new `.md` or `.txt` file instead. Have a different file type? Ask Bob: `Help me convert this file to .md format.`

---

### Option 2 — LinkedIn posts you've written

Paste **2–3 LinkedIn posts you've written** as `.txt` or `.md` files in this folder. These are public content, so there's no privacy concern. They give Bob a good sense of your voice, formality level, and sentence style.

---

### Option 3 — Write a reply to a sample email (no personal content needed)

This is the easiest option and requires nothing from your own files.

1. Open `sample-incoming-email.md` in this folder and read the email
2. Create a new file called `my-reply.md` in this same folder
3. Write your reply to that email as you normally would — casual or formal, brief or detailed, whatever feels natural
4. Save the file

Your reply becomes your tone reference. Bob will read it, understand how you write, and use that style when drafting.

---

## If you skip this entirely

Exercise 3 still works without any files in this folder — Bob will fall back to a neutral, professional tone instead of your personal voice.

---

## After the lab

Delete any files you added here if you'd prefer not to leave personal content in the workspace.
