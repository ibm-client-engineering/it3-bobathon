# Sample Incoming Email — Option 3 Tone Reference

> **Instructions for Option 3:** Read the email below, then write your reply in a new file called `my-reply.md` in this same folder. Write it exactly as you would in real life — your reply becomes your tone reference for Exercise 3.

---

**From:** Jamie Okafor (Project Manager, Cross-Functional Programs)
**To:** [Your Name]
**Subject:** Quick update on the Q4 initiative — where do things stand?

Hi [Your Name],

Hope your week is going well! I wanted to check in on the Q4 initiative before our steering committee meeting next Thursday. Last I heard you were working through a few open items — do you have a quick summary of where things stand and whether there's anything that needs escalation before we get in front of leadership?

No need for a long write-up — even a few bullet points would be really helpful so I can prep the deck.

Thanks so much,
Jamie

---

> **Tip:** Write your reply as you naturally would — don't over-think it. A few sentences is fine. Bob is looking for your tone, greeting style, level of formality, and sentence rhythm — not the content itself.
